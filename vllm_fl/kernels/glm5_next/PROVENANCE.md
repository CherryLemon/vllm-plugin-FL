# GLM5 sparse indexer attribution

The request/index metadata and cache wiring derive from the vLLM/GLM5-Next
integration and retain their SPDX Apache-2.0 attribution. Numerical K-pool,
FP8, bounded KDA and MHC broadcast implementations are maintained in
[FlagGems-vllm](https://github.com/flagos-ai/FlagGems-vllm) with their original
licenses and source attribution. The paged-MQA kernel derives from FlagGems.
